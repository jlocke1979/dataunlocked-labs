Course-adjusted performance

Compare rounds across Newman, Madison, Kellogg and other courses on the same scale.

Equivalent score translates each performance to a neutral par-72, slope-113 course. An equivalent 108 means “this was about the quality of a 108 on a standard course,” regardless of whether the actual score was 98 at Madison or 113 at Newman. For complete 18-hole rounds, differential = equivalent score − 72. For shorter rounds, the equivalent score is an estimated 18-hole pace using Golf Pad's course rating and slope; it is useful for trend analysis but is not an official WHS 9-hole differential.
