03 / Behind the work

A practical mind.
A curious point of view.

DATA Unlocked is the independent practice of Justin Locke, connecting business experience, data science and a love of making things understandable.

The commercial work helps organizations make better decisions. The open studio makes room for visual expression, civic questions and experiments that begin with “what if?”

Based in Peoria, Illinois. Background in strategy, pricing analytics and operations. MBA, University of Chicago Booth; graduate study in data science at Northwestern.

Start with a question.

A pricing decision, a report that needs rethinking, a public dataset worth exploring. The first step is defining what you want to understand.
